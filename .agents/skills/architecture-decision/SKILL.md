---
name: architecture-decision
description: Evaluate a dependency or architecture change and record an ADR.
---
State the concrete problem and evidence. Compare at least two options, including keeping the current simple design. Evaluate complexity, security, operations, learning and migration cost. Prefer simplest adequate option. Record Context, Options, Decision, Consequences and Revisit Criteria in `docs/adr/NNNN-short-title.md`. Do not implement unless asked.
